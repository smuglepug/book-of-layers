Your agency is a spectrum, not a trait

Layer 1

The Mind

Attention, working memory, imagination. The only layer that is entirely yours — and the foundation of every other one.

Layer 2

Body & Tools

Thought becomes kinetic action. Your brain remaps itself around the tools you use often enough.

Layer 3

Direct Interaction

First contact: reading other people's biology, space, and feedback in real time.

Layer 4

Direct Influence

One-to-one persuasion built on theory of mind and cognitive empathy.

Layer 5

Group Dynamics

Crowds do not follow arguments, they follow proof. Social proof and the first-follower principle.

Layer 6

The Uncontrollable

Nature, time, physics, other people's choices. Mastery here is knowing when to yield and adapt.
